How the estimates work

Uses a standard monthly amortizing loan with payments at the end of each month. For fixed-then-variable loans, the rate changes once after the fixed period and stays at the midpoint of your entered range thereafter. Your payment is recalculated when the rate changes, based on the remaining balance and your chosen payoff date. The payoff slider models a shorter amortization period, not a one-time lump-sum prepayment. Actual future variable rates may change repeatedly. Excludes fees, insurance, taxes, and early repayment penalties. Results are planning estimates, not bank quotes.
